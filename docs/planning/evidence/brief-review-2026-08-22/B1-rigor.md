# B1: scientific and statistical rigor review

Companions checked: register (rows A, C, D, E, F in full), test plan (full incl. 9-10), 08-10 brief (1.3-1.5, 3.8, 4-7, 13-16m, 27-32), vendor-comparison/README.md and runs/, evidence/skeleton-author-vendors/ (all five run dirs; per-record re-tally), recognition-protocol-pilot/results.md, mutation-per-request-pilot/README.md, architecture-respecification (via register), authoring-lessons-log (AL-328, AL-331, AL-482, AL-490..498), scripts/compare_skeleton_authors.py.

1. [CRITICAL] [4.1, F4] "DeepSeek V4 Pro emerged as the best judged prose" is contradicted by the cited artifact.
The only cross-lab judged panel (08-10 brief section 29, lines 2588-2597) scores v4-pro at judged quality -0.13 z, FIFTH of eight, behind Sonnet 5 (+0.69), Grok 4.6 (+0.61), GPT-5.6 (+0.38), Sonnet 4.6 (+0.14); section 31: v4-pro is "4.9x cheaper and gives up... 0.74 of judged quality". No later comparative panel exists (checked vendor-comparison/runs/, workplan, distillation plan, lessons log); the same folklore appears upstream in the test plan line 11. The true claim is best prose per dollar ($0.0398/book at fill rate 1.00). F4's model-per-stage argument is real, but its motivating fact inverts a measured quality ranking.
Recommendation: correct 4.1/F4 to "best value, mid-pack judged quality"; re-open the fill-model choice as the section 7 policy question the plan actually posed.

2. [CRITICAL] [4.2, F3, F4] The per-model product decisions rest on n=3 counts the experiment's own rules class as decision-inert; no valid inferential result exists anywhere in S-1.
The pre-registered primary (repair rounds, permutation test) is degenerate in both conditions: blind summary "statistic 2.571, p = 1.0000"; tool-assisted "statistic 0.000, p = 1.0000", and its summary.json says "every other field in this summary is exploratory and decision-inert per register row S-1". The plan's multiplicity discipline: exploratory endpoints "cannot trigger a decision rule"; 4.2's "Consequence for the product" triggers three. Computed on the records: fable 6/6 vs kimi 5/6 Fisher two-sided p=1.0; vs sonnet 4/6 p=0.45; vs haiku 3/6 p=0.18; any within-cell 3/3 vs 2/3 p=1.0; Wilson 95% CI for 3/3 is [0.44, 1.00]. Only two contrasts separate: tool vs blind (12/21 vs 2/21, p=0.0025) and v4-pro 0/6 vs fable/opus 6/6 (p=0.0022, just inside Bonferroni 0.05/21). "Frontier Anthropic tiers converge fastest and most reliably" (vs kimi 5/6) is statistically unsupported.
Recommendation: restate 4.2's verdicts as regime-level (supported) plus one model-level negative (v4-pro); mark the tier ranking as a screen needing replicates.

3. [CRITICAL] [1, F5, 4.3, 4.4] Every "reader" in the programme is an LLM rater; no validated perceptual instrument exists; the brief applies the 4.4 quarantine to only one of the claims it covers.
The predecessor corrected itself in print: "This section previously described its input as 'blind human annotation'. That was false. Every model evaluator here, and every evaluator anywhere in this document, was an LLM agent instance" (08-10 lines 1087-1091); its confidence table: quality confidence "low: no human or child has read any book" (line 2811). The 08-22 brief drops both disclosures and says "readers" in F6 and 4.4. Given S-0 failed its control and the six-question instrument compressed, the following stand on no validated instrument: finding 1's "read as different adventures / same book re-skinned" (support: 2 model raters, 3 pairs, one 26-node non-production graph, an effect both raters called "thin"); F5's same claim, where S-0's own data cuts the other way (all four raters called the D-7b bare-names pair, F5's passing configuration, the same adventure at scene 2); 4.3's "recognition unmoved/identical/landed earlier" refutations (S3/S5/S6/S7/S9), which used the older single-rater recognition measure the 08-10 brief itself says measures the wrong construct ("every number in section 4 needs re-reading", 6.4), on designs that held the defect layer constant by construction (5.3). These are "failed to detect with an unvalidated instrument", not "refuted"; genuinely refuted items are the deterministic ones (S8's structural_distance 0.0000 and 100% beat retention, M-4's gram counts, D-6/D-7 leak measurements).
Recommendation: add a provenance sentence ("all raters are LLM sessions; no child has read any book"); extend 4.4's unconfirmed marker to finding 1, F5's perceptual half, and the recognition-based rows of 4.3; split 4.3 into deterministic refutations vs undetected-with-broken-instrument.

4. [HIGH] [F3] F3's headline numbers do not match the artifacts or the brief's own table.
Re-tally of every record in e1r3-tools: cell A 12/21 (fable 3, opus 3, haiku 2, sonnet 1, kimi 2, flash 1, pro 0), cell D 14/20; the register's own itemization sums to 12 under a "14/21" header, so a register arithmetic slip was copied into the brief's central principle (the 14 apparently belongs to cell D). "Swept the harder 10-13 cell" contradicts the brief's own table (haiku 1/3, v4-pro 0/3 at D). Direction survives (12/21 vs 2/21, p=0.0025), but a checkable false count inside the largest-lever claim erodes trust in every uncheckable one.
Recommendation: correct F3 to 12/21 and "the four capable legs swept D"; rule: any count in the brief is regenerated from records, not register prose.

5. [HIGH] [4.2] The Anthropic-vs-rest comparison is confounded by transport, grader provenance, and format-failure scoring; the register's "tier-level conclusions only" caveat does not survive into the brief.
Registered limitation: subagent legs are "tier-labeled, not backend-pinned... tier-level conclusions only" with the serving snapshot "recorded per run" (plan section 10); no snapshot was recorded anywhere (e1r3-tools has no run.json; records carry input_tokens: null, latency_s: 0.0, finish_reasons: []). Mechanics differ per family: subagents iterate statefully and wield check_skeleton.py themselves inside the repo (isolation asserted, no transcripts committed to verify), while API legs are driven externally; DeepSeek/kimi losses are partly format and transport, scored as authoring failures. The sole grader is a checker whose policy rules and TAU_CELL floor were calibrated on the Claude-authored catalog, and the passing Anthropic shells sit nearest that catalog: min structural distance 0.051 (opus) and 0.066 (fable) against the 0.05 anti-clone floor, vs 0.15-0.20 for DeepSeek. "Which model satisfies our checker fastest" partly measures resemblance to the incumbent author.
Recommendation: carry the tier-level caveat into 4.2; commit subagent prompts/transcripts; report format failures separately from structural failures; note the checker-calibration circularity.

6. [HIGH] [4.5, F7] "Zero marginal cost" is a billing artifact; the winning leg's production cost is unmeasured; the 90%-of-spend comparison is not "the same grid".
Subagent legs bill nothing because they ride the owner's session; every token field in their records is null, so the claim is unmeasurable from artifacts; in production an Anthropic authoring stage pays API rates (the fill panel priced Sonnet 5 at $1.42/delivered book vs v4-pro $0.0398). 4.5's "90% of spend... against $1.30 for both DeepSeek legs across the same grid" compares an aborted 4-of-80-shell run plus smokes (premium legs burning censored blind repair loops) against the reduced 36-shell grid; the plan notes Gemini was "the only leg with a proven strict pass" in that phase. F7's "Haiku authored passing hard-band shells at zero marginal cost" overstates: exactly one hard-band Haiku pass exists (D r3), 1/3. The recommended architecture has no measured dollar cost at all.
Recommendation: run one costed Anthropic-API tool-assisted leg (or price from recorded session token telemetry); restate 4.5 with commensurable grids.

7. [HIGH] [4.2, F3] The blind-vs-tool-assisted contrast is real but bundles three variables, and the blind arm exists only at cell A.
Conditions differ in checker access, statefulness (stateless regenerate-per-round vs one continuous session), and budget metric (6 repair rounds vs 10 checker invocations; nothing establishes equivalence). F3's mechanism claim ("Structure authoring is tool-use") attributes the whole gap to the checker; a stateful no-tool session was never run, so statefulness alone could carry part of it. Blind cell D was descoped as "predictably censoring" (declared), so the harder cell has no blind baseline. The pass-rate reading survives censoring; the engineering attribution is under-determined.
Recommendation: one cheap ablation (stateful session, checker withheld) before F3 is treated as mechanism.

8. [MEDIUM] [F6, 5] Pre-registration is genuinely practiced but S-1's final shape is an adaptive design the brief presents as a planned two-condition experiment.
The register logs every amendment with data-contact statements (slate/grid revision after 4 seen shells; kimi leg and the entire tool-assisted condition added owner-directed after the blind cell-A interim, 14 of 15 censored, was known). Honest adaptive practice, but the tool condition was declared with endpoints only (no test, no alpha, no margins), and 4.2's opening ("Two conditions over the same premises, briefs, and strict bar") reads as symmetric and pre-planned. Precedents of acknowledged post hoc structure: D-3b fork subsets "applied after these labels existed"; D-7's 62% retracted because "the 62 percent method was never documented".
Recommendation: one sentence in 4.2 stating the tool condition's origin and endpoint-only registration; adopt the register's voiding language for S-1's original primary.

9. [MEDIUM] [whole brief] Multiplicity: discipline exists on paper, but every operative S-1 result is exploratory and the programme has had dozens of uncorrected looks.
The plan's guard ("10 pairwise leg comparisons x 5 endpoints x 4 cells guarantees spurious separation somewhere") is right, and the one pre-registered test returned p=1.0 twice; decisions then came from the exploratory grid. Across D-1..D-7b, Q-3..Q-3d, M-4, S0-S9, S-0..S-1, and six-plus vendor runs, endpoints number in the dozens with no family-wise control. Mitigation: surviving headline effects are enormous (5.9x, 24x, 127x, 120x) where multiplicity is immaterial. Danger zone: the small-margin claims the brief promotes (tier rankings, neutral-vs-diverge "within noise of each other at n=1", D-3c's collapsed magnitude).
Recommendation: a size threshold below which the brief will not report a comparison as an ordering (e.g., nothing under 3x at current n).

10. [MEDIUM] [4.2, 1] Generalization: verdicts measured on 2 of 17 cells, short lengths only, stated without the plan's own scope exclusions.
S-1 ran cells A (5-8 short) and D (10-13 short). 11. [MEDIUM] [preamble, 3.5, F4, 4.4] Evidence-class taxonomy: adequate in outline, violated at load-bearing points, missing the class the brief actually uses twice.
Violations of "every load-bearing claim names its class and artifact": finding 1 names a worked example, not a class or artifact; F4's "competent first-pass reviewer" and 3.5's V4 Flash claim cite "owner practice, 2026-08" (none of the three classes, no artifact) yet feed the product consequence; F5's perceptual half names no instrument. The taxonomy also fails to distinguish re-runnable deterministic (gram counts, checker) from unregenerable counted-from-records (run-6 "never can" report cost), or validated model-judged (D-5's Q6, D-4 tier 1) from never-validated (recognition; the panel's dialogue criterion that "is measuring nothing").
Recommendation: add "owner practice / anecdote, non-evidence" as an explicit fourth label; subscript model-judged claims as validated or unvalidated.

12. [MEDIUM] [4.2, 4.5] Reproducibility: deterministic re-scoring is excellent; generation-side metadata is absent for the winning legs.
A stranger can re-derive every pass/fail and distance from committed shells and records (done), the permutation seed is fixed (20260821), premises committed, API pinning exemplary. But no temperature or sampling parameter is set or recorded anywhere (grep -c temperature compare_skeleton_authors.py returns 0), the Anthropic legs have no snapshot, no committed prompts, no transcripts, no run.json; checker-run counts for v4-pro cell D missing from tools-meta.json. The S-1 table is verifiable but not repeatable: the tool-assisted Anthropic result cannot be re-run even approximately from the record.
Recommendation: commit emitted prompts, session snapshot ids, per-round checker logs for subagent legs; record sampling params for API legs.
