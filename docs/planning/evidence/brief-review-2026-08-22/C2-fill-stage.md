# C2: fill stage deep dive (3.3, 3.4, 4.1, F4, delivery half of F2)

Sources: brief in full; vendor-comparison/README.md, runs/deepseek-v4-pro-2026-08-20/, vendors*.json; deepseek-v4-pro-live-fill-plan; 08-10 brief 27-32; AL-172, AL-327, AL-328, AL-345, AL-420, AL-481, AL-490..498; UW-C307, UW-C315; register S-1; test plan; out/w4w5-pool/judgements.json; code: generation/ (orchestrator, skeleton_match, binding, prompts, variation, usage, fidelity, fidelity_gate, providers/{fallback,openrouter}, provider.py, worker.py, chunking), diversity/query.py, validator/ (reading_level, band_profile, continuity, consequence, walk), scripts/, core/config.py, skeletons/ (measured directly).

1. [CRITICAL] [4.1] "DeepSeek V4 Pro emerged as the best judged prose" is contradicted by the evidence it cites.
The judging behind 4.1 (08-10 section 29: 84 verdicts, 3 LLM judges from 3 labs, 4 books per leg on one band) scores V4 Pro -0.13 peers-only normalized, 5th of 8, behind sonnet-5 (+0.69, resting on n=1 delivered book), grok-4.6 (+0.61), gpt-5.6-sol (+0.38), sonnet-4.6 (+0.14). Section 31: grok "close to dominant"; V4 Pro 4.9x cheaper, "gives up... 0.74 of judged quality". What V4 Pro won is $/delivered book ($0.0398 vs $0.186-$0.277 Western: "a fifth the cost" is right). No later judging where V4 Pro topped exists (later V4 Pro runs: the live fill, judged by non-blind rung-7/8 subagent reviews that found serious defects; S-1 authoring, 0/6). Also "Six legs across five labs" describes the aborted vendors.json slate, which contained no DeepSeek leg; the measured slate was 8 legs across 6 labs.
Recommendation: restate 4.1 as "cheapest per delivered book at mid-table judged quality"; cite the cost axis the pick stands on, or re-run judging.

2. [CRITICAL] [4.1/F6] The V4 Pro selection rests on exactly the rater-produced ranking F6 forbids, with adverse deterministic evidence set aside.
F6: blinded raters "confirm, never produce, rankings"; 4.4 calls model-judged the weak class. The fill-model consequence is produced by that weak class (three LLM judges, no humans, no inter-judge agreement statistic anywhere in section 29; the dialogue criterion returned a near-constant, sd 0.19, scoring the zero-dialogue leg equal to the most-dialogue leg; the one pool with computed intervals, out/w4w5-pool/judgements.json, self-reports "supported": false, separated_pairs: 0). Deterministic evidence on V4 Pro is adverse: 38.9-52.9% delivery through a passing gate (AL-490); a (skeleton, brief) pair deterministically unfillable 7 of 7 via content filter (AL-492); frozen-field drift on every book; 0/6 as structure author. Human-gated backing: none ("no human or child has read any book"). The sourcing plan already hedges ("if v4 Pro is retained for prose quality despite its measured 38.9-52.9% delivery", section 7 P6).
Recommendation: pre-register the fill-model bake-off with fill rate as a covariate; let deterministic delivery plus cost carry the decision until a validated quality instrument exists.

3. [CRITICAL] [3.4/F2] The delivery floor is not wired into anything a production book passes through, and 0.6 licenses a permanent ~40% shortfall.
check_fill_integrity.py --min-fill-rate (default 0.6) is a standalone script: referenced in no workflow, no skill, no src/ module; UW-C307 says gate carriage is "still open". The 0.6 value is calibrated solely to split one incident (bad books 0.389-0.529 vs tightest known-good pair 0.635: margin 0.035); no reader-facing rationale for why 60% of commissioned words is acceptable. A book delivering 61% on every node passes the floor and production's per-node check (fidelity.py _WORD_COUNT_TOLERANCE = 0.4, lower bound exactly 60%, "not calibrated against real fill runs yet"); stage-1 exhaustion only downgrades to needs_review, never fails. Unit economics: you can pay full price for 61% books forever with no automated signal.
Recommendation: carry the fill-rate check in the gate or worker (the UW-C307 decision); derive the floor from band reading-time commitments (PL-23 already computes derived minutes) rather than one incident's gap.

4. [CRITICAL] [3.3/4.1] The differentiation directive does not even apply, at production settings, to the case measured at 24x budget.
build_differentiation_directive (generation/prompts.py:429) injects one drawn craft axis, one of four static paragraphs keyed to DifferentiationLevel, prior titles, and closed-vocabulary theme tags. The level comes from diversity/query.py::score_history, which is per-family and theme-gated: escalation requires this family's same-theme cell saturation to cross a ceiling. Cross-family same-skeleton siblings, the commercially load-bearing reuse case per UW-C315, always render TREE: "This structure is new to this family. Write it straight; no extra differentiation pressure applies", leaving only the axis draw (14 authored axes, ~7% collision chance). The AL-498 pair (deliberately distant themes) would land TREE even within one family. The committed delta experiment (shared-skeleton-pair-directed/differentiation.json) uses level=catalog plus opposed axes: a best case production would never emit cross-family, so even when it runs (blocked on network, UW-C315) it will not measure production behavior. The directive's text targets settings, cast, and imagery, while the measured 96.3/1000 shared grams are "generic connective prose", sentence skeletons the directive never addresses. check_sibling_fills.py is wired nowhere.
Recommendation: two compare_vendors.py --differentiation invocations of the same pair, one with the committed catalog spec, one with a TREE-level spec, reporting both deltas; then wire the sibling check into the worker for same-skeleton fills.

5. [HIGH] [3.3/F4] Production cannot express the conditions the V4 Pro evidence was collected under: unpinned OpenRouter plus a silent model-changing cascade.
All comparison legs ran pinned (allow_fallbacks: false); production build_provider (generation/provider.py:715-735) constructs the OpenRouter leg with no provider_order (no config field exists) and wraps it by default in FallbackProvider [primary, sonnet-4.6, ollama]. Consequences: (a) the plan's F2 latent defect is live on adoption: V4 Pro's slug has 18 endpoints with output ceilings 16,384-1,048,576 and mixed quantization while MODEL_OUTPUT_CAPS holds one slug-keyed row, reopening the AL-327/AL-328 truncate-parse-burn-repairs path; (b) the orchestrator resolves cap and chunking from the primary while a fallback leg may serve the call (FallbackProvider has no .model; UW-C271); (c) mid-book or mid-batch failover changes the author model silently: F4's per-stage selection has no runtime enforcement, and a chunked book can be written by two vendors with nothing flagging it (no per-book multi-model check).
Recommendation: land pinning and endpoint-aware caps before adopting 4.2's consequence; plan section 8.2 asked exactly this and the code answers "no pin".

6. [HIGH] [3.3] Selection is weighted random with a deliberate novelty floor, not "least recently used", and it guarantees decision-sequence repeats at exhaustion.
skeleton_match.py:552-672: rng.choices with weight 1/(1 + recent + 3*similar), multiplied up to 2x for theme overlap, explicitly never zero. A family with one recent use of a slug in a 3-slug cell redraws that same slug with ~20% probability. At Q-1 exhaustion all weights equalize and a full repeat of the decision sequence, the defect section 1 ranks most important, is guaranteed, mitigated only by finding 4's directive. S-4 is open.
Recommendation: fix the sentence; consider a hard LRU tie-break or a per-family per-cell reuse cap (UW-C315 floats this).

7. [HIGH] [3.3/3.4] Cross-chunk and cross-path prose coherence is unverified by anything in the gate; the two candidate validators are self-declared non-gates.
Chunked fills pass prose_so_far to each batch, but nothing verifies what that context bought: zero repair budget on the chunked path, merge whitelists only body and choice label, ending titles not re-themed (AL-494: one-shot and chunked books are contractually different). Post-hoc: validator/continuity.py is "a reported statistic, not a gate... not a gate candidate either" (flags 3,815 of 4,472 corpus nodes); consequence.py likewise; stage-1 semantic check is per-node beats adherence; check_prose_craft covers tense/moral tags/told emotion (tense the only whole-book signal, in a standalone script). Measured consequence: AL-490's book 2 asserts state a reachable path never established and "The gate passed it". For the 16+ tail the human approver is the only path-coherence check, and the run plan concedes a reviewer of a 632-node book "cannot read it as a whole".
Recommendation: say in 3.4 that path-level coherence is unmeasured; add the cheap detectors already proposed (AL-495 outbound CG-4 companion, AL-496 duplicate-body and POV checks).

8. [HIGH] [4.1/3.3] The catalog tail sits in the truncation regime the programme itself measured as unsafe, with whole-job-only failure recovery.
the-last-cartage (632 nodes, 49,953 commissioned words, 99,906 tokens) passes the feasibility screen at 95.3% of its ceiling while AL-328 measured truncation at 91% and AL-481 records that _FEASIBILITY_MARGIN = 0.8's "justification has quietly stopped holding at the top of the catalog". Live: one such fill cost $1.064 and 1,874s; its sibling brief burned 3x ~130s attempts against a deterministic content filter reported as "transient" (AL-492: a brief can be permanently unfillable with no signal). On the chunked path each batch re-sends the full skeleton plus prose-so-far (super-linear input cost), one failed batch fails the whole fill, and production has no resume below RQ whole-job retry: the countermeasures 4.5 cites exist only in the experiment harnesses, not generation/worker.py.
Recommendation: persist per-batch results for worker resume; surface finish_reason in job errors (AL-492's proposal); re-derive the feasibility margin from the current catalog.

9. [MEDIUM] [3.4] The reading-level gate is Flesch-Kincaid: arithmetic validated, construct not, never blocking, and demonstrably Goodharted once already.
validator/reading_level.py vendors FK; RL-13 is "always WARNING... never blocks"; bodies under 20 words skipped; the module documents FK misbehaving at 3-5 (negative grades). The syllable counter has a genuine known-answer test (AL-399: CMUdict, 115,901 words, residual bias -0.031) and band targets are single-sourced with a literature anchor, but no test scores known band-leveled children's texts against the windows: band validity asserted, not tested. The formula is an optimization target: Stage D repairs prose toward FK; AL-389/AL-394 record four drafting agents swapping regular verbs for irregular ones to satisfy the then-broken counter (live Goodhart). AL-491: in-band rate is confounded with fill rate (73.1% in-band at 42.7% delivery "conforms by accident"), and the vendor comparison's "in-band" axis inherits all of this.
Recommendation: report in-band only beside fill rate (AL-491's rule); add one leveled-text known-answer fixture per band.

10. [MEDIUM] [4.1/4.2] Repair economics are unmeasured in production, and the fidelity judge defaults to self-review.
Only harness-side repair figures exist (5 of 40 comparison books needed any repair; live run's passing books took zero repairs because under-delivery blocks nothing). No production fill has produced a repair-rate or repair-cost number, so F7's "repair loops with hard round caps" has caps (max_repairs=3 shared; 0 when chunked) but no measured cost contribution. Verified real where cited: near_cap and max_tokens stamping, preflight, --resume, HTTP 402 lesson, MODEL_OUTPUT_CAPS plus feasibility screen. Design smell: fidelity_gate.py defaults the semantic fidelity judge to prep_model, the model that wrote the fill (design #134), so the model that ignored words= also judges its own beat adherence at the stage deciding whether to spend a repair round.

11. [MEDIUM] [4.1] Even at face value, the judged-quality design cannot rank eight legs.
Four books per leg, one band, one brief per book; sonnet-5's +0.69 rests on one delivered book (3 verdicts); no inter-judge agreement computed; adjacent margins (+0.14 vs -0.13) inside plausible noise at n=4; run-6 showed a single degraded book moves a 4-book leg mean ~20 points. The harness's own ranking logic, where intervals exist, declines to support an ordering (w4w5-pool judgements: supported false). State n per leg; refuse ordinal language ("best", "emerged") the design cannot license; the register's one-primary-endpoint discipline was never applied to this comparison.

12. [MEDIUM] [3.3/F5] The running pipeline shares a prose-bearing layer (beats) that the programme's own D-6/D-7b evidence says leaks.
F5 is "load-bearing in the pipeline... none is aspiration", but 3.3's binding shares the skeleton's beats text (modulo slot tokens), ending titles, and the full decision sequence across every same-skeleton sibling (binding.py: render_bound_skeleton touches exactly those three surfaces). AL-490 measured bodies restating their beats at 0.51-0.668 content-word overlap, so shared beats are a sufficient transmission mechanism for AL-498's 96.3/1000 convergence, consistent with D-6. The stratified plan that would fix this is S-2, open. Until S-2 lands, F5 describes the target architecture, not the pipeline; 3.3 should say which stratum is shared today.

13. [LOW] [1] The headline scale figure is not reproducible from the committed catalog.
"677-node, ~118,000-word graph at 16+": the 677-node skeleton (the-tenfold-siege) commissions 42,233 fill words (body text incl. beats 22,665, labels 4,295); the largest commission anywhere is 49,953 words (the-last-cartage, 632 nodes). No basis yields ~118,000; "61 graphs and 11,458 nodes" also differs from the committed 84 files/15,470 nodes. Context-limit and tail-cost arguments scale with this number.
Recommendation: state the basis or correct it.

Strengths
- Metering engineered for honest failure: usage.py distinguishes unreported (None) from zero, counts unknown_calls, flags totals as lower bounds; fallback cascade attributes the billed leg; live-run harness reconciled to OpenRouter billing within 2%.
- The fill-rate floor's internal design is genuinely anti-gaming at the aggregate level: per-node capped credit means surplus cannot pay for empty nodes; the exploit it forecloses was measured real and is pinned by tests.
- Self-correcting evidence culture: run-6 fp4 retraction with drop-worst arithmetic; AL-327/328 wrong inferences corrected in place; both wrong content-filter diagnoses kept. Every audited number traced to a rerunnable artifact.

Top 3
1. Rewrite 4.1: V4 Pro is the cheapest delivered book ($0.0398, fill rate 1.00 in-harness), judged 5th of 8 on prose (-0.13); the selection stands on cost.
2. Move the delivery floor and sibling check into the path books actually travel (worker/gate); re-derive 0.6 from a reader-facing requirement; today a 61%-delivered book passes every automated defense.
3. Before shipping "fill with V4 Pro", land OpenRouter endpoint pinning plus endpoint-aware caps, and run the committed differentiation delta at production settings (cross-family TREE level), not just the committed best-case spec.
