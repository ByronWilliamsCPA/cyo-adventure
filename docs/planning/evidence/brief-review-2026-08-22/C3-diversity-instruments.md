1. [CRITICAL] [F5, 4.3, 4.4] The S-0 verdict data is adverse perceptual evidence against F5 itself, and the brief reports it only as an instrument failure.
S-0's known-answer design used the D-7/D-7b pairs, i.e. exactly F5's configuration (shared structure, independently authored decisional strata), as ground-truth "same adventure"; all four raters fired at scene 2 with distinctness 1, the scale's worst score (anchor "1 = one book in costumes"). The D-7b register row concedes the mechanism: 0/32 identical readings yet "agreement in meaning throughout", 11/35 choices sharing opening verbs, "the same acts are available at the same forks", "a reader study would be needed... no measure here can". So the programme's only rater data on the stratified plan says it reads as one book in costumes, while F5 is presented as earned on a wording metric (2.3/1000 grams). S-2's falsifier (b) (both raters same-adventure at position <=4 on the most-similar pair) is preordained to fire on any shared-armature pair per this same data: the plan's confirmation step and its favored architecture are on a collision course. Either raters are trustworthy (F5's perceptual half is in trouble) or they are not (S-0's validation design is uninformative); the brief takes neither horn.
Recommendation: state the adverse reading in 4.3/F5; before S-2's rater step add the decision-level variation mechanisms the re-spec names (premise varied per book, device repulsion), or re-derive known answers that do not presuppose S-2's outcome.

2. [CRITICAL] [1, 4.4] No instrument today detects the flagship defect, and the production request path is unguarded at the decision level.
D-4 tier 1, the one surviving computed measure, scores answer/device transfer over "every prop bound in a puzzle-carrying device category": puzzle-solution portability, a proper subset of the defect. Section 1's own worked example ("Open the door" / "Go around back") involves no device and is invisible to it; its validation is three pairs on one 26-node graph family, middle pair confounded (AL-185 collision), against Q6 ratings that are themselves "solution transfer" (agreement partly definitional). Production protections now: LRU-ish recency selection; the differentiation directive (wired, generation/worker.py:1018, delta never measured: UW-C315 "Still open: the delta measurement... and the pipeline wiring of check_sibling_fills.py"); check_sibling_fills imported nowhere in src/; the anti-template guard (moderation/leaf_diversity.py) advisory, fail-open, soft-FLAG-only, leaf-text distance, defined only for same-structure-fingerprint pairs (cross-skeleton decision sameness and same-decisions-different-words both pass); recognition broken; human approval sees one book and 08-10 1.5 says distinctness "cannot be evaluated on a single artifact". AL-498's 96.3/1000 same-skeleton pair "passed the deterministic gate". Honest rating: the property the business goal names is currently unmeasurable and unblockable in the pipeline; pre-launch status tempers user harm, not decision risk.
Recommendation: wire the sibling-gram gate (cheap, built), run the UW-C315 directive delta, fund a decision-level measure (finding 10).

5. [HIGH] [4.3/S-4] S-4 cannot see the defect it exists to measure, and its margins are structurally stacked.
Deciding endpoints are shared grams (D-7b passes it while S-0 raters score the pair distinctness 1), structural_distance (shape, which 1.3 says readers do not track; 20% rides on self-declared topology), and solution transfer (puzzle devices only): none captures same-acts-differently-worded. Margins ("shared grams lower by >=25%; structural_distance higher by >=0.05" vs S0 non-repeat pairs) ask generated arms to beat different-skeleton catalog pairs, plausibly near the 3.3 idiom floor where a 25% gram improvement is arithmetically unavailable, and penalize S2 for sharing topology, the thing F5 licenses. The S0 falsifier ("any same-skeleton ADJACENT pair recognized at <=4") is nearly unfireable: under recency weighting with 3-4 skeletons per cell the first repeat is pair (1,4), not adjacent, and confirmation runs "on adjacent pairs only" (plan E4), so the child's actual defect experience sits outside the confirmed pair set. If E4null fires this way, rule R2 locks in full reuse, the one arm whose only perceptual datum (P1, unconfirmed) is failure at position 3.
Recommendation: include the first-repeat pair explicitly, re-derive margins from measured baselines, add a decision-level endpoint before running.

6. [HIGH] [4.3 capital facts] Q-1's demand math exists nowhere; recomputing shows the R2 "depth purchase" remedy is large and insufficient.
No document states a request rate (grepped project-vision.md, tech-spec.md: nothing). Arithmetic: at one request/child/month in a preferred cell, exhaustion in 3-4 months; a no-repeat year needs ~12 skeletons/cell (3.3x depth), weekly cadence ~52/cell (14x); at S-5's amortization price (2 review-hours/skeleton) weekly-depth across 17 cells is ~1,600 review-hours/year. 08-10 section 22 shows depth cannot buy the outcome anyway: device vocabulary forces a repeated cipher form by book 6 (band 3-5: book 2) whatever the skeleton count; absent from 4.3. The brief also drops register Q-1's actual headline ("it is the wrong shelf to count": contract coverage 2/61 gates Q-2, the only mechanism ever measured to clear the anti-clone floor).
Recommendation: one-page demand model (requests/child/month x cell dwell -> strata-or-skeletons per cell per year) before any decision rule spends on depth.

8. [MEDIUM] [4.4/5] The recognition repair path fixes the symptom, not the construct; "two cheapest quality levers" is the wrong frame.
The cross-band control validates easy discrimination (band cues in vocabulary/sentence length) while every production use (S-2, S-4) is same-band; passing it never demonstrates the needed separation. The symmetric position rule is derived on seen data and re-imports 1.4's armature confound. A clean same-band control is constructible but not recoverable: it must be authored outside the catalog's converged mystery mode (sourcing-materials' own premise design rules are the recipe, unnamed by the repair path). Cheap in provider dollars, yes, but this is instrument repair, not a quality lever: it improves no book, and per finding 1 the branch it reopens fails by construction under current known-answer definitions.
