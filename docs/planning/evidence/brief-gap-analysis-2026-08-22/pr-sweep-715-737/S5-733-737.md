## #737: feat(generation): live structural rounds, eight owner rulings, and the ADR-011 amendment
**State**: MERGED as `41d30909` on 2026-08-22. *(Recorded as OPEN when written; corrected
2026-08-30. This is the commit that landed `AL-515`..`AL-527` and the eight owner rulings.)*

**What it did** (from `docs/planning/live-structural-round-2026-08-21.md`, the site of record):
Two live generation rounds hunting structural defects before bulk generation. Section 7.2
(differentiation directive makes shared 4-grams *worse*, 96.3 -> 110.7 per 1000 vs. a 4.0 budget)
and 7.6 (the mid-band fill-rate hole is vendor-shaped: sonnet-5 clears both the 0.6 floor and the
band target where v4-pro fails both, at ~7x unit cost) were pre-supplied. The rest:

### Section 7.1 (offline results)
- Mutation census reproduces the predecessor's findings exactly (validates the instrument) and
  extends the tally: across 6 committed v4-pro one-shot books, frozen-field mutation 2/6,
  ambiguous-field (title/ending-title) drift 5/6.
- Outbound-choice staging (`AL-495`/`UW-C312`) separates cleanly: 39 known-good committed fills
  score median 0.037 dangling rate; the 5 live v4-pro books score 0.690-0.854, a 20x gap tied to
  the fill-rate shortfall.
- Sibling convergence is structural, not vendor-specific: 70 committed same-band pairs median 1.2
  shared 4-grams/1000, but the one pair sharing a 551-node structure (two *deliberately* distinct
  hand-authored books) scores 202.0, 2x the DeepSeek same-skeleton pair's 96.3. Convergence is
  intrinsic to shared structure, not a DeepSeek idiom.
- Fill rate on one (skeleton, model) pair varies 42.9%-65.2% run to run: the 0.6 floor is a coin
  flip at this vendor's delivery variance.
- Narrative person is unpinned in the contract: gamebooks run 0.715-1.0 second-person, prose books
  0.0-0.27; same skeleton can legitimately land fills in different persons.

### Section 7.3 (Run C: chunked path, 3 failed takes before a live book)
- Take 1: `OpenRouterProvider` has no `.model` attribute; wrapper providers forward `None` via
  `getattr`, so `resolve_output_cap` silently resolves the permissive 131,072 default for every
  OpenRouter model: `MODEL_OUTPUT_CAPS` is dead config on that backend, and the chunked path can
  never engage there. Fixed at the harness boundary only; the adapter fix is deferred to a
  register row.
- Take 2: the chunked path has no *input* context accounting, it bounds output under the cap but
  not input+output against the endpoint's context window, and overflowed by exactly one token on
  a real run.
- Take 3: a 512-token probe passed an endpoint that then returned unusable output (empty mapping,
  non-JSON) on a real ~40k-token batch ask, pre-flight qualification needs a representative large
  ask, not a ping.
- Take 4: first live chunked book, and the *only* measured book with zero mutations of any kind
  (whitelist merge makes drift structurally impossible), but only 37.5% fill rate and 2.5% of
  nodes in reading-level band.

### Section 7.4 (Run B: ten-book grid across all six bands)
- Fill-rate defect is band-shaped, not constant: 3-5 over-delivers (117-125%), 16+ delivers
  fully, every band 5-8 through 13-16 delivers only 44-63%.
- Both tier-2 books (and only tier-2 books) mutated `variables[].description` (re-themed text
  only; names/types/bounds intact), the model "reliably rethemes every theme-bearing text field
  the contract happens to freeze," and the freeze mixes machine-critical and prose fields in one
  block.
- Outbound-staging defect isn't purely a fill-rate symptom: one book delivered 136% and still
  dangled 72.6% of outbound labels.
- Live cross-skeleton same-band pairs stay safely in budget (mean 1.27, max 3.30/1000 over 28
  pairs), all convergence risk is on same-skeleton reuse.

### Section 7.5 (tally and NO-GO)
Frozen-field mutation: 4/16 one-shot books (25%) mutated a frozen field; 15/16 (94%) mutated an
ambiguous one. **NO-GO on bulk one-shot story generation** on five ranked defects: (1) same-skeleton
reuse has no working lever, (2) mid-band fill-rate hole, (3) freeze contract can't say what's a
legal reskin, (4) 20-40% pair-failure rate reported as generic "transient failure," (5) chunked
path/cap machinery not production-ready on OpenRouter. Cost: ~$3.75 total (est. $2.85).

### The eight owner rulings
Sections 8 (three rulings, 2026-08-21) and 9 (five further rulings, 2026-08-21, plus 9.1's
2026-08-22 resolution), see the numbered list in the final reply.
