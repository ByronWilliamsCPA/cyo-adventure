## #727 "feat(generation): make every production skeleton fillable, and close two gate gaps that let unfillable books through"
**State**: merged 2026-08-17T17:39:15Z (created 2026-08-17, ~14 hours after #724/#726)

**What it did** (verified against `main` at merge, not just the PR body):
- `generation/skeleton.py`: raised the fill output cap from 32,000 to 131,072 tokens
  (`MAX_FILL_OUTPUT_TOKENS`/`MODEL_OUTPUT_CAPS`, queried from real per-model ceilings rather than
  assumed), and made `is_fill_feasible`/`expected_output_tokens` an actual selection filter
  rather than a logged-and-ignored predicate.
- `generation/skeleton_match.py` line ~193: `_production_candidates` now calls
  `is_fill_feasible(story, max_tokens=_FILL_MAX_TOKENS)` and excludes infeasible, non-production,
  and deprecated skeletons from selection - confirmed live on `main`.
- `generation/chunking.py` + `templates/fill_subset.md`: new breadth-first batched fill path
  (`plan_fill_batches`, whitelist-only `merge_fill_batch`) for a backend whose own ceiling clamps
  below a skeleton's need; wired into `generation/orchestrator.py` behind `is_fill_feasible` so
  every now-feasible skeleton still takes the original one-shot path.
- `validator/policy.py` + `validator/gate.py`: two new enforced gate checks, confirmed live on
  `main` -
  **PL-28** (the "MVP firewall": an ADR-011 MVP/Test seed can no longer be imported as a
  child-facing book; previously the selection layer excluded it but the manual import path did
  not, and three such seeds already had filled books in the corpus), and
  **PL-29** (per-band topology enforcement from ADR-011 s7, in `validator/topology.py`, shared
  with the offline mutation core; three skeletons declaring a topology their band forbids passed
  `check_skeleton --strict` clean and were previously caught only offline).
- Retired six legacy skeletons across three cells and added three new strict-authored skeletons
  under ADR-011 D11.

**Predicate status**: `docs/planning/unscheduled-work-register.md` line 259 (`UW-C07`) is now
marked **`done`**, dated 2026-08-17, stating explicitly: "Decided and done 2026-08-17: all three
[cap raise, chunking, retirement], in the order the owner directed... takes all 59 production
skeletons from 36 infeasible to 0."

**Impact on the analysis**: `SUPERSEDES`
- **Finding 2 (Fill delivery and feasibility)**, the specific sub-claims about "36 of 59
  production skeletons infeasible at the 32,000-token cap" and "13-16/16+ bands infeasible in
  their entirety" are **directly superseded**: the cap is now 131,072, all 59 production
  skeletons are feasible at that cap (0 infeasible), and both bands are selectable again. This
  is not a reinterpretation; the PR body and the register both give before/after counts (36->0,
  0/14 and 0/13 selectable -> both bands generate again).
- The **chunked-fill-specific** sub-claims in Finding 2 ("chunked fill re-sends the whole skeleton
  per batch, ~274k input tokens / 154k last batch against a 200k window on 2-3 skeletons" and
  "chunked path has zero repair budget") are **not** superseded, they are corroborated and
  reframed: the PR's own Notes section confirms chunking still re-sends the full skeleton per
  batch (input tokens grow "roughly quadratically in batch count") and that the chunked path
  "gets zero repair budget" by design. Additionally, the PR states the chunked path is now
  reached by **no** production request (every production skeleton is feasible at the new 131,072
  default), so on `main` as of this PR chunking is exercised only under a deliberately clamped
  backend - the analysis's chunked-path cost numbers describe a path that is currently dormant in
  production selection, worth a caveat if cited going forward.
- Two new gate rules (PL-28, PL-29) partially bear on **Finding 6 (Approval / `has_hard_block`)**
  and **Finding 5 (Safety stub)** only by analogy (both are enforcement-gap closures in the same
  gate) but do not touch `publishing/service.py` or `validator/safety.py` - those specific findings
  are unaffected (`NONE` for 5 and 6 specifically).
- Everything else (Findings 1, 3, 4, 7-15) is untouched: no change to structural convergence, the
  `--strict` skeleton bar enforcement question (strict census note in the PR is informational,
  correcting an earlier revision's arithmetic, not changing enforcement), walk-floor breaches,
  cost/runtime, job lifecycle, provider cascade, testing infra beyond what #724 already covered,
  instruments, model selection, reading level, register integrity, or ADR-023/028 composition.
