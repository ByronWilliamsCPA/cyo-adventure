# PR summaries: #719-#723 (ByronWilliamsCPA/cyo-adventure)

## #719 - feat(measurement): rebuild the syllable counter against ground truth, and close the W7 instrument
**State**: merged 2026-08-16T02:39:19Z (into `main` at `edfc3b3`, head `51c6bf3`, base `a73b46b`)

**What it did**:
- Rebuilt `_count_syllables` in `validator/reading_level.py` against CMUdict (115,901 unambiguously
  syllabified words) plus the project's own 390,334-token corpus. Accuracy: dictionary types
  83.03% -> 90.32%, corpus tokens 94.21% -> 99.14%, FK grade bias +0.268 -> -0.031. New
  `tests/unit/test_syllable_accuracy.py` pins ~40 known-answer word/syllable pairs by error class
  (regular `-ed`, `-ed` adjectival exceptions, `-Cle` syllabic, vowel-hiatus fixed/still-wrong,
  control words) plus three deliberately-wrong answers kept because fixing them would cost more
  words than it wins.
- Made RL-13's band tolerance one-sided (upper-bound-only) at bands `3-5` and `5-8` in both the
  gate (`validator/reading_level.py::_UPPER_BOUND_ONLY_BANDS`) and the generation-time repair loop
  (`generation/reading_level_loop.py`), reading the same constant so the two can't drift. Fixes a
  real defect: the old symmetric rule could accept a "simplification" that made young-band prose
  harder.
- Added `validator/imitable.py` (routes young-band endings with an imitable hazard to human
  review; router, not a gate) and `validator/blind_spots.py`/`validator/continuity.py` /
  `utils/sentences.py` as new modules; neither `imitable.py` nor `blind_spots.py` is imported or
  called anywhere else in `src/` or `scripts/` (verified by grep) - they exist but nothing invokes
  them.
- Closed the W7 measurement instrument: run 4, 43 arms x 2 judges, 86 scorings, $2.5855, first
  completed run after three container kills. Re-derived verdicts with `judge-gemini-3.1` dropped
  from the panel (5 KEEP / 1 RETIRE / 1 UNTESTED), and reframed run-to-run spread and
  false-positive rate per-criterion instead of pooled (`UW-C258`, `UW-C255` closed).
- Merged `main` in: 155 conflict hunks, including an **ID collision on both the lessons log and the
  work register** (main's `AL-351..361`/`UW-C248..252` used for different items than this branch's).
  Renumbered this branch's `AL-351..400` -> `AL-362..411` and `UW-C248..260` -> `UW-C253..265`
  across 322 references in 35 files. Explicitly notes: "no existing check would have caught the
  wrong resolution" - `check_lessons_log.py` would have reported "ok: well formed" even after a
  bad resolution silently dropped 11 lessons. Filed as new items `AL-413`/`UW-C266`.

**Impact on the analysis**: **UPDATES**

Justification, per finding:
- **Finding 13 (reading level)**. FK-variant and advisory-only are unchanged and confirmed still
  true directly from the shipped code: `validator/reading_level.py` docstring still says "RL-13 is
  advisory only and never blocks a story" and every finding is `Severity.WARNING`.
  The in-band/fill-rate confound is unchanged and is now explicitly documented in
  `BookReadingLevel`'s own docstring ("Unscorable nodes are excluded from both numerator and
  denominator, so a book with many short nodes reports a higher figure than a whole-book reading
  suggests") - acknowledged, not fixed. The "no ground truth" clause is now **partially stale**:
  #719 gives the syllable-counting sub-component real external ground truth (CMUdict) with
  known-answer test fixtures, closing that specific gap and eliminating the root cause of one
  concrete Goodharting incident (`AL-389`: four drafting agents swapped regular verbs for irregular
  ones to satisfy a band the old counter was misreporting). What #719 does **not** supply is
  ground truth that FK itself (or its band thresholds) tracks a real child's reading ability -
  the PR's own notes are explicit about this: "W12 is the only item that checks whether any of
  this correlates with a real child's reading; everything else here, including all of W7, measures
  internal consistency." So: instrument-level ground truth closed, product-level ground truth gap
  stands exactly as before.
- **Finding 14 (register integrity)**. #719 is itself a second, self-documented instance of the
  same defect: it rewrote the IDs of already-published lesson/register rows during a merge (not
  the `bf7cad1` incident the finding names, but the same class of event), and states plainly that
  no existing tooling would have caught a wrong resolution. This corroborates the finding with
  fresh, first-party evidence rather than fixing it - `check_lessons_log.py` was not hardened

- **Finding 7 (cost)**. Changing the repair loop's band symmetry could plausibly change iteration
  count, but does not touch the uncapped-call-count or per-call-cost structure the finding is
  about. The reading-level loop's cost share is independently re-measured post-#719 in
  `docs/planning/cyo-brief-gap-analysis-2026-08-22.md` ("38%, 51%, 59% of a book's bill... for an
  `in_band` result of 0.155") at the same 38-59% order of magnitude the finding cites - i.e. the
  cost problem is confirmed, not resolved, by the current code.
- **Finding 5 (safety)**. `imitable.py` and `blind_spots.py`, both named "uncalled" in the finding,
  are literally introduced to `main` by #719's merge commit and remain uncalled today (verified by
  grep) - the finding accurately describes the post-#719 state; #719 is the origin of the artifact
  the finding is about, not a fix to it.
- All other findings (F5's structural-stratum claim, fill delivery/word-count tolerance, skeleton
