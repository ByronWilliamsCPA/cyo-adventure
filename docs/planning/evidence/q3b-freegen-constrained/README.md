# Q-3b: skeleton-free generation with the constraint set stated

Reruns Q-3 (`16i`) with the three constraint classes its authors were never given:
opening-decision floor, arc floor, ending schema, plus a branch-depth ceiling and a
band reading-level target. Six graphs, generated by mutually isolated authors, none of
which read any existing skeleton, contract or story in this repository. One pass, no
repair round, no self-review prompt.

`validate.py` checks all thirteen stated constraints independently rather than trusting
any author's self-report; `scripts/check_graph_structure.py` is the second, independent
opinion. The two agree on all six.

## Result

| | first pass |
| --- | --- |
| structurally clean | **6 of 6** |
| repair rounds needed | **0** |
| band-budget failure class (opening floor, arc floor, ending schema) | **eliminated, 0 of 6** |
| reading level at or under the band edge | **1 of 6** |

Nodes 28 to 32, endings 6 to 8, forks 7 to 22, shortest positive path 10 to 15,
longest path 11 to 16. Every graph honoured every structural and budget constraint.

## The split that matters

**Constraints a model can verify by tracing its own artifact, it meets. Constraints
requiring a computed statistic it cannot estimate, it misses.** Structure, reachability,
ending mix, path lengths: 6 of 6. Reading level, stated in the same prompt as an explicit
Flesch-Kincaid 4-6 target: 5 of 6 over the 7.0 gate, and no book put more than 48 percent
of its nodes inside the band. Stating a constraint is sufficient for the first class and
useless for the second, so the reading-level repair loop is mandatory rather than an
optimisation.

## Premise convergence, unchanged and worse than `16i` reported

Six authors who shared nothing produced **five coastal lighthouse-or-lantern stories with
fog or a storm and an elder keeper**:

| | title | archetype |
| --- | --- | --- |
| g1 | The Lighthouse of the Whispering Bell | coast, storm, bell, elder |
| g2 | The Kite Race of Windmere | **the one outlier** |
| g3 | The Lantern Keeper's Apprentice | coast, fog, lighthouse, elder |
| g4 | The Light of Salt Hollow | coast, storm, lighthouse, elder |
| g5 | The Lantern of Fogbound Marsh | marsh, fog, vanished keeper, elder |
| g6 | The Bell of Whistling Cove | coast, storm, bell |

Convergence is not lexical: median 1.56 shared four-grams per 1000 bodies-only, 11 of 15
pairs at or below the 3.3 generator floor. Sentence independence is free when nothing is
shared, exactly as `16j` predicts. But **4 of 15 pairs breach the 4.0 budget and every
breach is a same-archetype pair** (g4/g6 at 9.0, g4/g5 at 5.0, g1/g5 at 4.9), which is
the content-driven breach `16i` predicted and this run reproduces.

Removing the plan buys wording independence and does nothing whatever about the premise.
Premise scheduling is therefore mandatory in every architecture, not a property of any one
of them.
